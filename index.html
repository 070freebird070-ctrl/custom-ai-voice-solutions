Built to stay in bounds

  • Every call is answered with a clear notice: it's an AI assistant, and the call is recorded
  • Inbound only — we never make cold calls or send marketing blasts
  • Text messages go only to people who called you first and asked for them; STOP always works
  • Your prices come from your sheet — the assistant can't invent a quote
  • No voice is ever cloned without its owner's verified, written consent
  • One switch turns the whole thing off, any time
