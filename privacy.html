Privacy, in plain words

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Who we are

Custom AI Voice Solutions is a founder-run service in Dallas, Texas that builds and operates AI phone assistants. Contact: guygemini070@gmail.com.

What we collect when you call our demo line

  • Your phone number (caller ID)
  • A recording and a written transcript of the call
  • Whatever you choose to tell the assistant — typically your first name, a callback number, and what you're interested in

Please don't share sensitive information (card numbers, passwords, codes, health details) with the assistant — it will ask you not to, and we don't want it.

Who processes it

Calls are carried and processed by third-party providers: our telephone carrier (Twilio), our AI voice platform (ElevenLabs), and the AI language-model providers that power the conversation. Those providers receive and may retain call audio and transcripts under their own terms in order to operate the service.

What we use it for

To hear and answer your inquiry, to call you back if you asked us to, and to review and improve how the assistant handles calls. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We aim to keep demo-line recordings and transcripts only as long as needed to follow up and review quality — our target is no more than 30 days on the AI platform, with our own working copies kept only while your inquiry is active.

Deleting your data

Email guygemini070@gmail.com from any address, tell us the number you called from and roughly when, and we'll delete our copies and request deletion from our providers, subject to legal and provider limitations.

The obvious things, stated anyway

  • Every AI call starts with a notice that it's an AI and that the call is recorded and shared with the providers above.
  • We're an independent service — not affiliated with or endorsed by any AI platform vendor.
  • If our practices change, this page changes first.
